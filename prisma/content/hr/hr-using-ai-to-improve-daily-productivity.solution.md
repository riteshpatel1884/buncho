**What the interviewer is really testing**

The interviewer wants to know if you look for ways to optimize your workflow or if you rely on AI as a crutch.

**Expert answer**

> I view AI as a highly efficient administrative assistant rather than a primary creator. I use tools like ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot to automate time-consuming, routine tasks such as formatting raw text data, drafting basic email templates, or brainstorming initial ideas. By offloading these foundational steps to AI, I free up roughly 20% of my week, which I redirect toward deep strategic planning, complex problem-solving, and collaborating directly with my team.

**Why this works**

The answer frames AI as an assistant, gives specific tasks, quantifies the gain (about 20% of the week) and says where the saved time goes. It shows optimisation without dependence.

**Tips to make it yours**

- Name two or three concrete routine tasks you hand to AI, not just "I use ChatGPT".
- Say what you do with the time you save, and who stays accountable for the result.
