**What the interviewer is really testing**

To see if you understand the balance between human emotional intelligence and automation.

**Expert answer**

> AI is exceptional at pattern recognition and quick data retrieval, but it completely lacks emotional intelligence, contextual empathy, and genuine relationship-building. In this role, success relies heavily on understanding the subtle, unstated needs of our clients and navigating complex team dynamics. AI can generate data reports, but it cannot sit across from a frustrated client, truly listen to their concerns, read their body language, and build a lasting, trust-based human partnership.

**Why this works**

It is balanced: it admits AI's strengths, then names specific human strengths and links them to a concrete scene in the job instead of making a general claim.

**Tips to make it yours**

- Start by crediting what AI does well, so the answer does not sound defensive.
- Tie the human strengths (judgement, empathy, trust) to the actual duties of this role.
