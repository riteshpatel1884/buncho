**Formula: Motivation → Evidence → Fit → Contribution (60 to 90 seconds)**

1. **Why AI engineering (motivation):** what draws you: turning powerful models into reliable products people use; the mix of software engineering, data and product judgement; solving hard trade-offs (quality, latency, cost, safety).
2. **Evidence:** projects, coursework, open source, internships or papers that show sustained interest (what you built, what you measured, what failed and what you learned).
3. **Why this role/company (research it first):** specific product, domain, scale, technical challenges, culture, mission. Mention something concrete from their blog, docs, product or job description.
4. **Fit and contribution:** skills you bring (evaluation, RAG, agents, deployment) and how they map to the team's needs; what you want to learn.
5. **Close with growth/impact:** how the role supports your direction.

**Template**

"I'm drawn to AI engineering because [what you enjoy]. In [project], I [built/measured X], which taught me [lesson]. I'm interested in [Company/team] because [specific reason: product, problem, scale, values], especially [detail from their work]. My strengths in [skills] map to [team need], and I'd like to grow in [area]."

**Do:** be specific, honest and positive; connect to the job description; show curiosity about their problems; have 1 or 2 questions ready.

**Avoid:** generic "AI is the future", salary/prestige as the lead reason, bashing current employer, or reciting your résumé. If you lack professional experience, lean on projects, depth of learning and ability to ship.
