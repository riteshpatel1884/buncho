The **EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)** is the first comprehensive AI law. It is **risk-based** and applies to providers and deployers whose AI is placed on the EU market or whose output is used in the EU (extraterritorial).

| Tier | Examples | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Unacceptable risk** | Social scoring, manipulative/exploitative AI, untargeted face scraping, emotion recognition at work/school, most real-time remote biometric ID in public | **Banned** |
| **High risk** | Hiring, credit scoring, education scoring, essential services, law enforcement, migration, critical infrastructure, medical-device AI | Strict requirements + conformity assessment |
| **Limited risk (transparency)** | Chatbots, deepfakes, AI-generated content | Disclose AI interaction, label synthetic media |
| **Minimal risk** | Spam filters, games | No obligations (voluntary codes) |
| **General-purpose AI (GPAI)** | Foundation/LLM providers | Technical documentation, copyright policy, training-data summary; extra duties for **systemic-risk** models (evaluations, red teaming, incident reporting, cybersecurity) |

**High-risk obligations** (provider): risk management system, data governance (representative, error-checked data), technical documentation, automatic **logging**, transparency/instructions for use, **human oversight**, accuracy/robustness/cybersecurity, quality management system, CE marking, EU database registration, post-market monitoring and serious-incident reporting. **Deployers**: use per instructions, monitor, keep logs, inform affected people, fundamental-rights impact assessment in some cases.

**Timeline (phased):** prohibitions and AI-literacy duties from Feb 2025; GPAI rules from Aug 2025; most high-risk obligations from Aug 2026-2027 (check current status, as amendments are being discussed).

**Penalties:** up to EUR 35M or 7% of global turnover for prohibited practices; lower tiers for other breaches.

**Engineering impact:** inventory and classify systems; build documentation, evaluation and logging into pipelines; dataset governance and bias testing; human-in-the-loop UX; transparency labels and watermarking; vendor due diligence for GPAI; monitoring and incident process.
